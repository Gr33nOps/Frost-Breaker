# Frost Breaker

**Short description:** Break the frost, find the lava, and cross ten pixel-art levels with five lives.

Journey through a frozen valley, from the forest gate to the frost crown. Aim your returns, work around solid rock, and uncover one to three guarded lava bricks arranged within each formation.

Every lava brick adds ten seconds of piercing power to your remaining time. The countdown pauses with the game and clears on a lost life or a new level. Five lives must carry you through the entire journey.

- Ten handcrafted formations: pines, peaks, a bridge, a castle and a crown.
- Pixel-art scenery, stone controls, animated frost fragments and molten effects.
- Original chiptune music and arcade sounds.
- Mouse and keyboard controls, with a locally saved best score.

**Controls:** Mouse or arrows to move. Click or Space to launch. Escape to pause.

Play in your desktop browser, or download the Windows version and run `Play.cmd` after extracting the ZIP.
