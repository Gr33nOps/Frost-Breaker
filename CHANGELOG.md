# Changelog

## 2.1.0

- Arranged one to three protected lava bricks per level, with symmetric pairs and three-brick patterns in larger formations.
- Made each lava pickup grant ten seconds of piercing power, added to any time remaining.
- Added a sidebar countdown that freezes while paused and clears on a lost life or new level.
- Kept contact timing accurate when lava expires during a physics step.
- Refreshed normal-ball gameplay screenshots and the compact ten-level gallery.

## 2.0.0

- Added a browser edition and itch.io HTML upload ZIP.
- Added a portable Windows download with runtime libraries and a game icon.
- Refined swept ball collisions, rounded brick corners and moving paddle returns.
- Added mouse movement and click-to-launch alongside keyboard controls.
- Built a ten-level campaign with five shared lives, permanent rock obstacles and one guarded lava brick per level.
- Limited lava to the current life and level, with a fresh normal ball on every serve.
- Added original pixel-art assets, editable sources, animations, music and sound effects.
- Unified live game text with the openly licensed Jersey 10 pixel font.
- Added screenshots, cover art, credits, checksums and automated regression checks.
